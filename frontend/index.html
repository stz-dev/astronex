Welcome to AstroNex!

AstroNex is an educational website created by Team TWD as part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It explores the history of space exploration through the stories of spacecraft, Mars rovers, satellites, and other missions.

Discover their technologies, scientific achievements, and historical milestones. Explore 3D models, learn through interactive mini-games, and uncover the stories behind these incredible machines.

Our mission is to make space exploration accessible, engaging, and unforgettable.
