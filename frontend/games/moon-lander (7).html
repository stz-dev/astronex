Touchdown!

★★★

The real story
  • 1967Surveyor 3. Shiny rocks fooled its landing radar, the engines kept firing and the probe bounced twice. In 1969 the Apollo 12 crew landed about 180 m away and took parts home.
  • 1969Apollo 11. Computer alarm 1202 rang during the descent. Armstrong took manual control and landed with only seconds of fuel left.
  • 1971–72Apollo rovers. Folded inside the lander, three Moon buggies drove the surface. All three are still parked there.
  • 2019Chang'e 4. The first probe to land on the far side of the Moon, where Earth never shows in the sky. A relay satellite carried its signal home.

Your mission: set a rover down as gently as those landers had to.

How to play

  1. Hold the screen (or Space) to fire the thruster. It slows you down but burns fuel. On phones, hold the THRUST button.
  2. Aim left or right with the mouse, ← →, or by sliding a finger on the screen.
  3. Land on the green pad slower than 10 m/s, with no sideways drift. It glows red until you are right above it. Real Apollo legs were built for about 3 m/s.
  4. Dark side: there is no light, so your altitude and the pad are hidden. You get 3 flashes (F, right-click or the FLASH button). Each one shows the ground and your height for 1 second, then the computer keeps a dim mark where the pad is.
  5. The faster you fall, the harder the engine pushes, but the more fuel it burns. Hits hurt more at high speed: smoke, then alarm and glitchy steering, like Apollo 11's 1202.

The real story
  • 1975Soyuz 18a. The third stage failed to separate, so the crew aborted. The capsule landed in the mountains and its parachute snagged some trees, which stopped it about 150 m from a cliff.
  • 1983Soyuz T-10-1. A fire broke out on the pad. The escape tower fired seconds before the rocket exploded and pulled the capsule away.
  • 2018Soyuz MS-10. A side booster hit the core stage. The escape system fired and the crew landed about 20 minutes after launch.

Four emergencies can hit your launch: engine failure, off course, bad stage separation or fire on the pad.

How to play

  1. Watch the launch. One of four emergencies strikes and the alarm sounds.
  2. Solve three sums before the timer runs out to free the capsule. Tap an answer or press 1–4. A wrong answer costs 3 seconds.
  3. Steer the parachute with the mouse, ← → or by sliding a finger. Dodge birds and falling debris.
  4. Land on the green zone, a sea or a safe field. Trees, towers and tall buildings destroy the capsule.
