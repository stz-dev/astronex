Abandoned but not Forgotten

Spacecraft left on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space do not disappear from history. ASTRONEX gathers them in one place: every model, date and story is a reminder that their work keeps inspiring people. Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge.

The NASA challenge

Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026). The challenge asks teams to tell the stories of equipment that NASA left behind on the Moon and Mars, and to make people care about it.

  • Storytelling. Every spacecraft has its own story page: what it was for, what happened to it, why it still matters.
  • Moon and Mars. The archive covers equipment left on the Moon (Apollo lunar module, Surveyor 3), on Mars (Spirit and Opportunity, Sojourner, Viking 1, Phoenix, InSight, Ingenuity) and in deep space (Pioneer 10, Dawn).
  • Validity. Facts, dates and numbers come from NASA, JPL and other official sources. Each of the main exhibit pages ends with an OFFICIAL SOURCES list with a direct link to every page used, and we separate confirmed facts from interpretation.
  • Creativity and engagement. 3D models you can rotate, Interactive Explore, signal-travel-time tool and games.
  • Citations and rights. All outside resources are cited. NASA images are credited to NASA / JPL-Caltech. No other copyrighted material is used without permission.
  • Language. The whole project is in English.

We follow the NASA Space Apps Challenge rules and judging criteria (Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation). This project is not endorsed by NASA.

Use of AI

The team used Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to help write and edit the code, the exhibit texts and the scripts that generate some 3D models. The team directed the work and reviewed it. Facts in the exhibit texts were checked against the NASA and JPL sources listed on each page.

Sources

3D models resource: Sketchfab was used in this project.

  • NASA NSSDC — mission facts, dates and last-contact records.
  • NASA Science — mission pages (Pioneer 10, Dawn, InSight, Ingenuity, Mars Pathfinder, Viking and others).
  • NASA JPL — mission status reports, the Dawn Journal and press releases.
  • NASA 3D Resources and Photojournal — reference models and images.
  • Sketchfab — used as a source of 3D models and as a reference for model shapes.
  • Signal travel times: distances at the date of last contact computed from planetary ephemerides (astronomy-engine); Pioneer 10 and Dawn figures taken from NASA / JPL statements.

Images and 3D models: NASA / JPL-Caltech. This project is not endorsed by NASA.
